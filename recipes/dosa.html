Dosa

A dosa is a classic South Indian savory crepe made from a fermented batter of rice, lentils, and fenugreek seeds. Cooked thin and crispy on a hot griddle (tawa), it is naturally gluten-free and usually served piping hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

INGREDIENTS:

  1. Rice (3 cups): Raw rice (like Sona Masuri) or idli rice provides structure and volume.
  2. Urad Dal (1 cup): White, hulled black gram is essential; it binds the mixture and aids in natural fermentation.
  3. It sChana Dal (2 to 3 tablespoons): Adds a subtle nutty flavor, body, and a nice golden-brown color.
  4. Methi Seeds (1 teaspoon): Fenugreek seeds kickstart fermentation and add flavor.
  5. Poha (1/2 to 1 cup): Flattened rice is soaked and ground with the batter; it gives the dosa a crispy exterior and a soft, tender inside.
  6. Water & Salt (1/2 teaspoon): Water is needed for soaking and grinding, while non-iodized sea salt is added after fermentation for seasoning.
  7. Oil or Ghee: Used for greasing and drizzling around the edges while cooking.
