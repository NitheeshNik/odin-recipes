Chicken Curry

DESCRIPTION

Chicken curry is a globally popular dish featuring tender chicken pieces simmered in a spiced gravy. Originating in the Indian subcontinent, the sauce typically builds on a rich base of browned onions, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, and a blend of aromatic spices like turmeric, cumin, and coriander.

INGREDIENT

  1. Chicken: 500g (1 lb), cut into bite-size pieces (thighs or breasts work best).
  2. Onions: 1 large or 2 small, finely chopped.
  3. Garlic: 2-3 cloves, minced.
  4. Ginger: 1-2 teaspoons, freshly grated or minced.
  5. Green chilies: 1-2, slit (optional, for heat).
