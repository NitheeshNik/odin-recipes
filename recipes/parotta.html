Parotta

DESCRIPTION

A parotta (or porotta) is a layered, unleavened flatbread from South India and Sri Lanka. Made from maida (all-purpose flour), water, salt, and fat, the dough is stretched paper-thin, pleated, coiled, and cooked on a hot griddle with plenty of oil or ghee to create a uniquely soft, flaky texture.

INGREDIENT

  1. All-Purpose Flour (Maida): 2 to 3O.
  2. cupsWater: \(\sim \frac{1}{2}\) to \(\frac{3}{4}\) cup (lukewarm is best for dough elasticity).
  3. Salt: 1 to 1.5 teaspoons.
  4. Sugar: 1 teaspoon.
  5. il or Ghee: 3 to 4 tablespoons (plus extra for soaking and pan-frying).
